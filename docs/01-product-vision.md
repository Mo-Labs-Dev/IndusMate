# IndusMate Product Vision

## Tagline

**We take care of your industry.**

## Product Definition

IndusMate is a virtual industry platform that enables organizations to design,
operate, monitor, maintain and improve their factories from one intelligent
digital workspace.

It connects factories, production units, machines, PLCs, sensors, cameras,
projects, employees, documents and maintenance activities in one platform.

## Vision

Create an intuitive virtual industrial environment where managers, engineers
and shop-floor employees can understand and manage their work without
struggling with complicated software.

## Mission

Make industrial software simple, visual, intelligent and accessible while
remaining secure and reliable for real industrial environments.

## Core Promise

Every IndusMate feature must make industrial work easier, clearer or safer.
